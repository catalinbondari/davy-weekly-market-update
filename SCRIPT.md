## 2. The 10-year — Five per cent

*Mon 21 – Thu 24 Sep · about 0:41*

So the ten-year. Five point one eight per cent, up seven basis points on Thursday alone, and a full percentage point this year.

Inflation expectations haven't moved. People just want more to lend to the US government, and on Wednesday a five-year auction had the weakest demand since 2018.

Then on Thursday a Fed official said another hike might be needed.
