## 2. The 10-year — Five per cent

*Mon 21 – Thu 24 Sep · about 0:54*

Turning to the ten-year itself: five point one eight per cent, up seven basis points on Thursday and a full percentage point since the start of the year.

What matters is the composition. Over the past year the real yield — the return after inflation — has risen from one point seven five to two point seven six per cent. That accounts for the entire move; inflation expectations are essentially unchanged. Investors are demanding more to lend to the US government, and Wednesday's five-year auction drew the weakest demand since 2018.

Thursday added to the pressure, with the New York Fed's John Williams saying a further hike may be appropriate. And this is a global move: German yields are at their highest since 2009, and Japan's since 1996.

## 3. The war — Hormuz

*Since 28 Feb · about 0:50*

Behind the move in yields is the war, now in its seventh month. Gulf crude exports are running at eight to nine million barrels a day, against around fifteen million before the conflict.

This week showed how headline-driven the market has become. Brent fell to ninety-nine dollars on Tuesday on hopes for the UN talks, rose almost four per cent on Wednesday when they failed to deliver, and a further three point four per cent on Thursday after Houthi missile attacks on Saudi Arabia. It settled at a hundred and six sixty.

On the diplomatic side, Iran has proposed a sixty-day ceasefire, and President Trump expects a deal after the midterms on the third of November.

## 4. Oil to rates — The transmission

*Latest prints · about 0:37*

So how does a conflict in the Gulf become a five per cent yield? Through inflation. US energy prices are sixteen per cent higher than a year ago, and petrol twenty-seven. Headline inflation is three point four per cent in the US and three point two in the euro area.

All well above the two per cent target. That is why the Fed raised rates on the sixteenth of September, and why markets now price roughly a seventy per cent chance of a further hike in October.
